RE9 REAR CARRIER - CAMEL LAND ROVER

Build 42, single-player and multiplayer. Enable Justyns vehicle Pack;
both vehicles are included.

Park the motorcycle near the Camel Discovery. Turn the motorcycle engine off
and have both riders dismount. Stand outside near either vehicle and open the
vehicle radial menu (V by default). Choose "Load motorcycle onto rear carrier".

The original motorcycle rides sideways, raised behind the rear bumper, with no
extra rack model. Its parts, fuel and storage contents stay on the same vehicle.
Its current weight is added to the Land Rover. Riders cannot enter it while mounted.

To unload, stop the Land Rover, stand outside near it and choose "Unload motorcycle
from rear carrier". Leave a clear 3-by-3 area behind the car. The bike is put back
on the ground and the car's original chassis mass is restored. Mounted pairs
reconnect from their saved part data when the world loads.

Other vehicles keep standard towing. In multiplayer, every client and the
server must have the same mod version. Loading and unloading are validated
on the server. Mounted pairing is saved in replicated vehicle-part data.
Clients follow the car locally for a steady display; the server maintains
the actual bike position and mass. Unloading replicates the ground position.

Placement defaults live in media/lua/shared/emre_re9_carrier.lua, C.Offset:
physics metres, x across the car, y above its chassis origin, z along its length.
Single-player and server/client logic tests passed using the installed game's
Lua runtime with mocked vehicle/network APIs, including observer clients,
late joins, ownership changes, saved pairing and stale unload requests.
An actual hosted/dedicated driving session remains to be checked in-game.
