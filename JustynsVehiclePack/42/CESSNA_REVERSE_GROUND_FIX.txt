CESSNA REVERSE GROUND SUPPORT - 1.0.33

C172Phys.holdGroundGear now supplies bounded upward support if the body sinks
more than 6 cm below its normal tire-plane height. The wheel drivetrain and
existing pitch controller remain active. Support scales with frame time and
vehicle mass, releases as the body rises, and never resets position, heading,
or velocity. It is limited to a 50 cm recovery band near the gear datum.
The ground-mode caller excludes flight and parked/frozen aircraft.

Changed code: 42/media/lua/client/Cessna172/C172Phys.lua

Validation: compiled by the installed game's Lua compiler; actual B42 Lua
regressions passed for bounded upward support, normal suspension clearance,
upward release, frame-time scaling, steering preservation, unchanged flight
gains, 73 recovery headings, stationary migration, and propeller pivot poses.
Isolated native flat-ground probes exercised forward and reverse taxiing;
they do not reproduce a complete game session or multiplayer carrier loads.
Graphical in-game reverse validation is still required.
