CESSNA RESIZE / COCKPIT UPDATE - PACK 1.0.23

The approved aircraft and attached equipment are 20% larger in every axis.
The mounted Tenere remains its normal in-game size, centered between the
main wheels with a slight mounting angle and refitted ropes. Mounted luggage
is hidden. The actual motorcycle remains physics-disabled and non-colliding;
its mass is not added to the aircraft. Rear loading/unloading moves to 5.4 m
behind the aircraft, and the aircraft candidate search covers that position.

The seats move rearward and lower inside the cabin. Pilot and passenger idle
and exit clips use forward-bent legs and matching clothing helper bones.
The steering column extends toward the pilot and the palms meet its grips.
Only the game characters are rendered: browser review mannequins, cutaway
geometry and roof guides are excluded from exported aircraft assets.

Suspension positions/radii and recovery height follow the uniform aircraft
scale. Inertia follows squared dimensions; calibrated ground attitude control
keeps the taildragger near 13 degrees. Engine-off parking latches until engine
start, towing, flight or authority change and reasserts native static state
each render tick to resist controller wakes and solver jitter.
The centered spinning propeller, black/striped blade paint, engine-controlled
red/green wingtip lights and bright roof/stock headlights are retained.
The invisible motorcycle placeholder now has a proper named DirectX frame.
