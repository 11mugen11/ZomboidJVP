CESSNA HANDLING UPDATE - PACK 1.0.22

Red port and green starboard wingtip lenses glow whenever the engine runs,
independently of the landing/taxi/headlight switch. Every client follows the
standard replicated engine state; fixed lens overlays use the existing depth
shader with lamp emission enabled and do not create large colored ground pools.

A stationary engine-off aircraft receives a parking hold. The physics owner
releases it when the engine starts. Moving dead-stick landings are allowed to
roll; the hold is limited to walking pace, grounded height and low vertical speed.
Native towing releases the hold. Ground attitude torque is skipped while parked.

The mounted Tenere now has a point-sized interaction footprint. Its old negative
extents still formed a large polygon over the aircraft doors. Native loading,
inventory retention and the existing tiny lateral collision placeholder remain.

Healthy landings keep their native momentum and transform. The final approach
blends toward the taildragger stance and the handoff waits until close to the
tire plane. Exceptional sunk/tipped recovery restores horizontal momentum through
the existing physical impulse channel instead of a display-only speed setter.

The landing rollout coasts toward a stop at about 2.3 km/h per second. Steering
remains available. Throttle, S/brake or handbrake ends coast assistance immediately
and restores normal wheel control; brakes can stop it faster. A collision/blocked
roll cancels assistance so it does not keep pushing against an obstacle.

Restart the game after installing. Host and clients need the same release.

VALIDATION: actual B42 Lua compilation and behavior fixtures, native import of
both lamp meshes, dedicated-server carrier load/unload and cabin-side polygon
clearance, engine/headlight-independent lens visibility, parking ownership/restart,
healthy touchdown momentum, safety recovery impulses, coasting and pedal takeover.
The prior fixed propeller hub and angled recovery checks remain passing. Existing
Tenere/Discovery carrier regressions are retained. A graphical landing/entry session
and actual two-client multiplayer session have not been run by this update.
