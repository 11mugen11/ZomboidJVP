TENERE FUEL ECONOMY - PACK 1.0.32

Healthy tank capacity: 23 liters (about 6.1 US gallons).
Nominal road economy: 50 US MPG, approximately 4.70 L/100km.
Calculated healthy-tank range: 304 miles / 489 km at normal road speeds.
Highway use increases gradually above 80 km/h (about 46 MPG at 130 km/h).
Idling consumes 0.4 liters per real hour. Engine wear, damaged-tank leakage
and the server's CarGasConsumption setting can reduce the range.

Tenere-only native part callbacks replace the generic car calculation that
penalizes low chassis mass. Fuel is consumed on the server or in singleplayer;
clients do not add fuel back. Elapsed game minutes are converted using the
configured day length so road range is independent of day length. Both mounted
profiles retain capacity and consume no fuel. Other vehicles are unchanged.

New Tenere tanks are a dedicated repairable 23L item. Saved installed 18L tanks
are upgraded in place, retaining inventory identity, condition and actual fuel.
No free fuel is added. Capacity still decreases when the tank is damaged.

Install on clients AND server for multiplayer fuel behavior; restart them.
This release was installed locally only. The production server was not changed.
Range is calculated and callback-tested, not a completed live road endurance run.
See .validation/tenere-fuel-economy in the development workspace for checks.
