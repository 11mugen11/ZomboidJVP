CESSNA UNDERSIDE TENERE CARRIER - PACK 1.0.20

Park a normal Tenere behind the aircraft (within two tiles of a point 4.5 tiles
behind its center), stop both vehicles, dismount and turn off the motorcycle.
Open the normal V vehicle menu anywhere within its usual aircraft interaction
range. Select Mount Tenere under aircraft. Use Unload Tenere behind aircraft
to release it into a clear 3-by-3 area at the same rear staging point.

The aircraft must be landed and stationary. Loading/unloading is checked by
the server. A moving, occupied, already paired or rope-towed motorcycle cannot
be loaded. The aircraft carrier accepts the Tenere; the Discovery rear carrier
still supports both existing adventure motorcycles with its existing behavior.

Approved display: rectangular hull-fitted belly rack, transverse bars, sideways
Tenere with its fork/front wheel/fender turned 32 degrees, revised lateral fit,
rear frame tie and double front-fork rope wrap. Motorcycle luggage is hidden
while mounted and returns on release. Its actual items, inventory containers,
condition, fuel and engine tuning are retained, not replaced with a new bike.

The displayed motorcycle is a conditional aircraft model. Its real vehicle
follows invisibly without a native towing joint, with all tire raycasts removed
and object contacts disabled. Its required valid tiny physics placeholder is
positioned 2.20m laterally outside the aircraft fuselage collision box; this is
not an empty-body or globally collision-free engine patch. Mounting adds no bike
or luggage mass to the aircraft and does not change flight tuning. Native tire
maintenance callbacks cannot re-enable mounted wheel contacts.

Both host and clients need this release and a full game restart to load the new
models/texture atlas. Pair records, native vehicle IDs and mechanical-ID checks
are retained. Observers, streamed-in planes and saved pairs independently show
the mounted display; delayed release packets cannot display a stale old pair.

Propeller blades are black with a narrow yellow band and white/red outer tips.
The separate spinning mesh and original pivot are preserved. Existing bright,
wide landing/taxi/rack beams and red port/green starboard lights are retained.
Render model scale is normalized so the vehicle scale is applied once, keeping
the visible airframe, wheel diameters and mounted display aligned with physics.
