/*
    Cessna 172 - AIRCRAFT PART ITEMS.

    These exist so the aircraft's own components are REAL, functional vehicle parts rather
    than renamed car parts or decorative rows. A VehiclePart only becomes installable,
    uninstallable and repairable if it declares an `itemType`, and that item is what
    actually carries the part's condition through a save, a server restart and a
    multiplayer session - VehiclePart.getCondition() reads it back off the inventory item.
    So "make the aircraft parts actually function" is, mechanically, "give them items".

    SCOPE. Every id here is C172-prefixed and lives only in this mod. Nothing overrides or
    extends a vanilla item, no vanilla item's DisplayName is touched (which would rename it
    on every vehicle in the game), and no vanilla vehicle can install any of these - a part
    can only accept the itemType its own script names.

    DISTRIBUTION IS DELIBERATELY NOT DECLARED. None of these appear in loot. That is the
    correct answer for aircraft spares in Knox County: a Cessna's ailerons are not in a
    hardware store. They come off ANOTHER Cessna - uninstall from a wreck, install on
    yours - which is real salvage gameplay and needs no distribution table. The airframe
    items are also repairable in place (see cessna172_fixing.txt), so a damaged aircraft is
    always recoverable without one.

    ICONS are vanilla icon names that vanilla items already use, so every one of them
    resolves out of the shipped texture packs. No icon ships with this mod and none can be
    missing.

    ConditionMax = 100 across the board, matching vanilla vehicle parts: the mechanics
    panel, the tooltips and the HUD all speak in whole percent, and a part with a smaller
    ConditionMax would quantise its bar into visible jumps.
*/
