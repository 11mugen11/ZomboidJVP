CESSNA GEAR / PROPELLER FIX - PACK 1.0.21

The taildragger parks nose-up at approximately 13 degrees. Suspension support
stations are calibrated to that visible tire plane; the paired hidden tail rays
are spread for taxi stability. Belly collision clearance prevents the fuselage
from supporting the aircraft before its tailwheel. Ground pitch/roll damping
uses torque and leaves yaw steering and linear velocity free.

Recovery and touchdown seating now use the angled tire plane, keeping heading.
On the local driver's first entry, a stationary grounded aircraft is reseated
once to repair the previous gear pose. Moving or airborne aircraft are skipped.
After restarting, exit and re-enter your parked plane. RIGHT AIRCRAFT remains
available if a previously saved aircraft is too far out of position to migrate.

Propeller pivot compensation now uses the part's local scale, with vehicle scale
applied once by the parent transform. Its hub stays centered while the blades spin.

Validated using actual B42 Lua, 73 recovery headings, 72 propeller rotation poses,
and a native flat-terrain parked/taxi fixture. Tire offsets in that fixture were
within 2cm of the visible ground plane; taxi covered 6.7m. The native fixture
supplies floor chunks and stubs only the Java lazy-world callback, without changing
the installed game. Graphical taxi/flight and live multiplayer remain untested.

The Tenere loading/unloading implementation is retained.
