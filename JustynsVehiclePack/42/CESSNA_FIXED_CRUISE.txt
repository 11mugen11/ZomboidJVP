Cessna fixed-height cruise - v1.0.36

Red, Blue, Green and Yellow use the same flight controller.
After takeoff the aircraft climbs gradually to one fixed world body height: 86 game physics metres.
This sits above the standard map's building floor range (highest logical floor 31).
Cruise no longer follows roof, hill or lake surface probes. The camera follows physical height
continuously instead of stepping between terrain levels. The HUD retains its existing display scale;
its displayed feet are not literal physics metres converted to feet.

Powered healthy flight holds cruise height without juggling throttle. Engine failure, stalls and
damaged controls still affect flight. Choose Land Aircraft from the V menu for a controlled descent.
The approach eases into its existing flare and wheel handoff; aborting landing resumes the climb.
Terrain probes remain available for landing support and outdoor lighting.

Install the same version on the server and every client, then fully restart them (including shaders).
Existing parked aircraft and all four spawn IDs remain supported.

Validation: production flight kinematics at 20/30/60/120 FPS; changing terrain samples and elevated
takeoffs; full descent, engine-off glide; camera pin/unpin restoration; occupant depth signal;
parking/menu, touchdown and reverse support regressions; actual game Lua compilation.
High-altitude graphics and a live flight with multiple players still require in-game verification.
