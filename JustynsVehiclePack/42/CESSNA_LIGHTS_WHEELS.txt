CESSNA LAMPS / ROLLING WHEELS - PACK 1.0.24

Red port and green starboard glow comes from the actual modeled wingtip
lenses whenever the aircraft engine runs. It is independent of reverse
gear and the headlight switch. The old colored automotive reversing-light
sources are black/disabled, preventing detached colored glows.

Viewpoint replaces custom vehicle shaders with its own materials. The
Cessna therefore drives standard emission switches from engine state for
its navigation lenses. This changes only the aircraft's visual stop-lamp
flag, not brake input, braking force, engine state, or other vehicles.

Both roof lamps now live in the primary aircraft mesh, with real lens UV
areas and only their forward glass faces marked as lamps. Project Viewpoint
can detect their actual origins and parked directions rather than inventing
fallback car-headlight positions. The detector from the installed Viewpoint
JAR was run directly against the exported mesh/textures and found both lamps.
A fixed lower-cowling lamp lens shares the landing-light switch and the roof
pair's forward illumination. Old source-atlas light patches are disabled.

Landing/taxi source intensity rises to 2.8 / 2.4, and reach to 110 / 90 game
units. The local installation also widens Viewpoint's beam-width preference
from 20 to 40 degrees per side; this Viewpoint preference affects all vehicle
headlights. Existing Viewpoint aim, brightness and reach multipliers are kept.
For a manual installation, set Debug > Headlights > Beam width to 40 in
Viewpoint as well. First- and third-person use that same lighting pipeline.

The three visible wheels, including hubs, bolts and rust details, are separate
render assemblies. They roll at ground speed divided by actual tire radius,
reverse with backward travel, keep their hubs on the fixed axles, and freeze
when parked or airborne. Each aircraft has private visual transforms, so
nearby aircraft cannot animate each other's wheels. Native wheels, suspension,
collision, aircraft mass, flight controls and the mounted Tenere are retained.

Fully restart Project Zomboid after installing. Host/server and clients need
the same mod release. The local Viewpoint beam-width preference is separate
from the mod files and is client-side.

VALIDATED: 35 native mesh imports, atlas UV/index limits, actual Viewpoint
lamp detection/aim, 360 hub-pivot poses, ground/reverse/parked/local+remote
airborne animation cases, engine-only lamp-switch cases, B42 Lua compilation,
and native tire-contact/taxi/propeller/recovery regression fixtures. Night-time
graphical gameplay and a live two-client session have not been performed.
