YELLOW HONDA ACTY FUEL TRUCK -- Build 42.21
Pack: EmreAdventureVehiclePack_B42, version 1.0.38
Vehicle script: Base.JVP_ActyFuelTruck

No additional mods are required. The required Acty and Tank Water assets are
bundled under separate names. The normal Acty vehicle is not replaced.

The 1200 L Tank Water model is permanently mounted in the bed. Its usable fuel
capacity is 2000 litres. It starts empty. It has no water/fluid-mixing interface.
The engine fuel tank remains separate and is used only to drive the truck.

FILLING THE TRUCK
Right-click the truck and open either:
  ENGINE fuel tank: fill the truck's driving reserve from cans or a station.
  EXTERNAL bed tank - PETROL ONLY: fill the 2000 L tank from a powered station.
Park the bed's rear interaction area within normal station range (2 tiles per
axis). Station fuel and electricity requirements are the game's normal rules.
Turn the engine off when filling the driving reserve.

DISPENSING
Start the Acty's engine and leave it running. Park a receiving vehicle with its
fuel inlet within 4 tiles per axis of the Acty's rear bed interaction area.
This doubles the vanilla pump's -2..2 square search to -4..4, on the same floor.
Right-click the receiving vehicle, or select it under the Acty's EXTERNAL tank
menu. The option always identifies the external bed tank as its source.
Generators can be selected from the same menu or by right-clicking a nearby
generator. Switch the receiving vehicle/generator off first. No hose item,
generator, electrical connection or separate pump fuel is required for the Acty.
Use Escape or move to cancel. Flow is 8 L per real second. The pump stops when
the bed tank is empty, the destination is full, the Acty engine stops, either
vehicle moves away, the operator leaves, or the operator disconnects.

There is no siphon/dispense option for this truck's engine fuel tank. The bed
tank cannot refill the same truck's engine tank through the pumping menu.
The occupied bed retains only 10 storage capacity.

SPAWNING
Find "Honda Acty Fuel Truck (Yellow, 2000 L)" in the debug/admin vehicle spawner,
or use Base.JVP_ActyFuelTruck. It also spawns rarely in newly generated parking,
medium/good vehicle zones and junkyards. Existing Actys are not converted.

MULTIPLAYER
Install the same complete pack on the server AND all clients. Restart the game
and server after updating. Keep the existing mod ID EmreAdventureVehiclePack_B42.
All transfers run on the server, debit the dedicated bed part, clamp the amount
to available fuel/free space, and transmit changed parts/generators to clients.
Clients send requests, not fuel quantities. State is rechecked every transfer.
Concurrent users share the real remaining supply; no client writes fuel levels.
The bed fuel is saved with the vehicle part and persists through normal saves.
