Justyns vehicle Pack
===========================
Combined from the supplied EmreRE9Motorcycle (0.2.22), Land Rover Discovery
(lrdisco1), and Cessna172 (1.10.4) mod folders. Original vehicle/model/item
identities and component code are retained. Source mod metadata is in credits/.

Cessna mod author: RentCollectorClips. Aircraft model: "Plane" by osmosikum,
CC BY 4.0. See credits/Cessna172-CREDITS.txt for full attribution and links.
The supplied motorcycle audio code attributes its samples to a user-supplied
Backcountry ATV archive. The supplied motorcycle and Land Rover metadata do
not identify their original authors; their metadata and existing code credits
are preserved without replacing them with a new authorship claim.

Bundle changes: RE9 speed/audio and case capacities; black Camel body paint,
removed side Camel logos and hood badge, dark steel rims and 450 trunk capacity; Cessna
yellow body with black main-wing tips and 800 baggage capacity; multiplayer
rear RE9 carrier with a mounted collision profile and static/render/chunk
following; fitted pickup-derived Rover cabin and restored transparent windows.
New Rover interior geometry adapts The Indie Stone's base-game pickup truck
dashboard, steering wheel and seats. Its texture is referenced from the game.
Packaging merges translations and sandbox settings under one B42 mod ID.

Yamaha Tenere 700 World Rally Edition 2023 model/textures: supplied by the
user in TENERE as OBJ/FBX/BLEND/STL ZIP archives. The archive does not include
an author/license statement. Original source archives are retained unchanged
in the workspace. Conversion, material atlas and game adaptation are included
in this update; model authorship is not reassigned.

Honda Acty fuel truck addition: original 1989 Honda Acty Attack Truck by TheSquidd,
Workshop 3571246232. Tank mesh and texture: original 1200 L Tank Water by
DarylMasterGG, Workshop 3652517636. Adapted locally for Justyn's personal pack.
Original authors retain ownership. Bundling here does not grant permission to
publish or redistribute these assets. Tank Water's original license is retained.
